Rosary Chapel – Privacy Policy

Rosary Chapel is a prayer app. It is designed to collect nothing about you.

What we collect

Nothing. Rosary Chapel has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not use any third-party services that collect data.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings (prayer style, voice, reminders).
  • The personal intentions you type.
  • Your prayer history (number of Rosaries prayed and your streak).

This information is stored only on your phone and is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

Rosary Chapel is a one-off purchase made through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies; we never see or receive your payment details.

Audio and notifications

All prayer audio is built into the app and plays offline. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; they are not sent from a server. On Android, the app may use the phone's own text-to-speech engine as a fallback for a line without built-in audio; that runs on your device.

Permissions

Notifications are used only for the reminders you switch on and, on Android, for the playback controls while a prayer is playing. You can turn them off at any time in your phone's settings.

Children

Rosary Chapel is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
